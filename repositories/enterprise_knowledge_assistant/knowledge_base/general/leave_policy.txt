ZENSAR TECHNOLOGIES - EMPLOYEE LEAVE & TIME-OFF POLICY

Document Type: Human Resources Policy
Organization: Zensar Technologies Limited (RPG Enterprises)
Version: 3.1
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Purpose and Scope
This policy defines the leave entitlements and application procedures for all full-time permanent employees and probationers at Zensar Technologies.

2. Annual Paid Leave (Earned / Privilege Leave)
- Entitlement: All full-time permanent employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year (January 1 to December 31).
- Accrual Rate: Leave is credited on a pro-rata basis at 1.67 days per completed calendar month of active service.
- Carry Forward: Up to 15 days of unused earned leave can be carried forward to the subsequent calendar year. Accumulation is capped at a maximum of 45 days.
- Encashment: Unused earned leave is encashable upon separation or retirement as per company policy.
- Notice Requirement: Planned annual leave of 3 or more consecutive days requires prior approval in the Zensar HRMS at least 7 calendar days in advance.

3. Sick and Medical Leave
- Entitlement: Full-time employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year, available from day one of employment.
- Medical Certificate: A valid medical practitioner certificate is mandatory for sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive days.
- Carry Forward: Unused sick leave cannot be encashed or carried forward.

4. Casual / Personal Leave
- Entitlement: Full-time employees receive 5 days of casual leave per calendar year for unforeseen personal matters.
- Usage: Can be taken for 1 or 2 days at a time; cannot be combined with annual paid leave.

5. Summary of Standard Annual Paid Leaves
Permanent full-time Zensar employees receive a total of 35 days of paid leaves per year:
- 20 Days Earned / Annual Leave
- 10 Days Sick Leave
- 5 Days Casual Leave

6. Parental & Special Leaves
- Maternity Leave: 26 weeks of fully paid maternity leave for eligible female associates.
- Paternity Leave: 10 working days of paid paternity leave, usable within 6 months of child birth.
- Bereavement Leave: 5 consecutive working days of paid leave in the event of death of an immediate family member.
- Marriage Leave: 3 consecutive working days of paid leave for an employee's wedding.
