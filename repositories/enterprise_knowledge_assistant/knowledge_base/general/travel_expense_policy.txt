ZENSAR TECHNOLOGIES - TRAVEL & EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY

Document Type: Finance & Operations Policy
Organization: Zensar Technologies Limited (RPG Enterprises)
Version: 3.1
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Purpose and Guidelines
This policy governs official domestic and international business travel for Zensar employees, ensuring safety, fiscal responsibility, and transparent reimbursement.

2. Domestic Travel Guidelines (India)
- Air Travel: Economy class bookings through the corporate travel desk for travel distances exceeding 500 km.
- Rail Travel: 2nd AC / 3rd AC train bookings for approved domestic routes.
- Hotel Accommodation: Approved 3-star/4-star corporate partner hotels booked via the Zensar Travel Portal.
- Daily Per Diem & Meal Allowance: Up to INR 1,500 per day for meals and incidental expenses in Tier-1 cities (Mumbai, NCR, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune).

3. International Travel Guidelines
- Air Travel: Economy class on designated international carriers. Business class is eligible for flights exceeding 9 continuous hours with VP approval.
- International Per Diem: Fixed daily allowance according to country tier ($75/day in USA, £65/day in UK, €70/day in EU, R800/day in South Africa) covering meals and incidentals.
- Travel Insurance: Comprehensive global medical and travel insurance provided for the duration of the international deployment.

4. Expense Reimbursement Workflow
- Submission Window: Expense claims with original GST receipts must be submitted via Zensar ERP within 15 days of travel completion.
- Approval Hierarchy: Manager approval followed by Finance audit; disbursements processed in the next payroll cycle.
