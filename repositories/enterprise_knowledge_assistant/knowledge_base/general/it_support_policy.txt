ZENSAR TECHNOLOGIES - IT SUPPORT, HARDWARE & SERVICE DESK POLICY

Document Type: IT Infrastructure & Support Policy
Organization: Zensar Technologies Limited (RPG Enterprises)
Version: 3.1
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Global IT Helpdesk & Support Channels
Zensar Global IT Infrastructure Services provides 24/7 technical support for hardware, software, VPN, cloud accounts, and network connectivity:
- Internal Service Portal: ithelpdesk.zensar.com (or via Enterprise Knowledge Assistant MCP ticketing)
- IT Support Email: ithelpdesk@zensar.com
- Emergency IT Hotline: Extension 4357 (HELP) on office VoIP, or +91-20-6605-4357

2. Hardware Allocation and Refresh Cycles
- Laptop Allocation: Standard high-performance enterprise laptops (Dell Latitude / Lenovo ThinkPad / Apple MacBook Pro based on engineering role requirements).
- Hardware Refresh: Corporate hardware is refreshed every 36 to 48 months of active deployment.
- Peripherals: Employees receive an external monitor, mouse, keyboard, and headset for approved hybrid setups.

3. Software Access and Licenses
- Approved Software: All software must be installed via the Zensar Software Center or approved IT service catalog.
- Unauthorized Software: Installing unlicensed, cracked, or unauthorized peer-to-peer software on company assets is strictly prohibited.

4. IT Service Level Agreements (SLAs) for Ticket Resolution
- Critical (P1 - System/VPN Outage for Multiple Users): Response within 15 minutes; Resolution target within 2 hours.
- High (P2 - Individual Machine Breakdown / Critical Client Blocker): Response within 30 minutes; Resolution target within 4 hours.
- Medium (P3 - Software Installation, Access Requests): Response within 2 hours; Resolution target within 24 hours.
- Low (P4 - General Inquiries, Peripheral Requests): Response within 4 hours; Resolution target within 48 hours.

5. Ticketing Integration with MCP
Employees can utilize the Enterprise Knowledge Assistant to directly raise and monitor IT tickets stored in the enterprise ticketing system.
