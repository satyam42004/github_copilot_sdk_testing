ZENSAR TECHNOLOGIES - CYBERSECURITY & INFORMATION SECURITY POLICY

Document Type: Enterprise Information Security Policy
Organization: Zensar Technologies Limited (RPG Enterprises)
Version: 3.1
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Information Security Governance & Standards
Zensar Technologies enforces strict information security and risk management aligned with international industry frameworks:
- Certifications: ISO/IEC 27001:2022, SOC 2 Type II, and GDPR / Indian DPDP Act compliance.
- Zero Trust Architecture: Access is verified per request with Least Privilege access controls.

2. Access Control, Passwords & Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA): Mandatory MFA across all corporate accounts, VPNs, AWS/Azure consoles, and email portals (using Microsoft Authenticator or hardware security keys).
- Password Complexity: Minimum 12 characters, requiring uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and special symbols; mandatory expiration every 90 days.
- Screen Lock: Inactive workstations must auto-lock after 5 minutes of inactivity.

3. Data Classification & Confidentiality
- Public: Marketing materials and public press releases.
- Internal: General company communications and employee handbooks.
- Confidential: Client source code, project architectures, test databases, and contracts.
- Restricted: Sensitive PII, customer credentials, financial records, and cryptographic private keys.

4. Incident Management & Reporting
Any suspected phishing, unauthorized data access, malware detection, or loss of corporate devices must be reported within 1 hour to security@zensar.com or the 24/7 Security Operations Center (SOC).
