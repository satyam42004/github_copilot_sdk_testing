1. Organization Summary & Background
Zensar Technologies Limited is a leading digital solutions and technology services company, part of the multi-billion-dollar RPG Enterprises conglomerate. With over 10,000 employees globally, Zensar partners with enterprise clients across North America, the UK, Europe, and South Africa to deliver transformative digital experiences, scalable cloud engineering, artificial intelligence platforms, and next-generation application modernization.

2. Parent Group: RPG Enterprises
Founded by Dr. Rama Prasad Goenka in 1979, RPG Enterprises is one of India's premier industrial conglomerates. Zensar operates under the flagship leadership of RPG Enterprises, adhering to core group values of ethics, innovation, and "Hello Happiness".

3. Core Corporate Values (RPG Philosophy)
- Passion: Relentless pursuit of excellence in digital engineering and client outcomes.
- Trust & Integrity: Highest transparency in business operations, client confidentiality, and corporate governance.
- Agility: Speed, adaptability, and responsiveness in adopting emerging technologies such as Generative AI, LangGraph agentic workflows, and cloud-native solutions.
- Hello Happiness: Fostering employee wellbeing, psychological safety, and meaningful career enrichment.
